Subject: Warehouse Incident – Bengaluru Distribution Center

Dear Operations Team,

A water leakage incident has temporarily restricted access to a section
of the Bengaluru Distribution Center.

The affected section is expected to remain unavailable for approximately
3 days beginning September 6, 2026.

Inventory stored in the affected section may not be available for outbound
fulfilment during this period.

Warehouse operations are assessing the affected inventory and will provide
a further update.

Please review open customer orders assigned to this warehouse and identify
orders that may require stock reallocation.
